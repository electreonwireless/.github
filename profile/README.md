# Electreon Wireless: Charging the Future of Mobility ⚡️🛣️

Welcome to the official GitHub organization for **Electreon**. We are the leading provider of wireless charging solutions for electric vehicles (EVs), dedicated to accelerating the global transition toward a carbon-free, electric transportation system.

## 🌍 Our Mission
At Electreon, we believe that for electric mobility to achieve 100% global adoption, charging must be invisible, seamless, and continuous. Our mission is to eliminate range anxiety and reduce the environmental impact of heavy batteries by turning roads into shared, wireless energy platforms. 

Through our **Wireless Electric Road Systems (ERS)**, we enable vehicles to charge while driving, idling, or parked—lowering costs for fleet operators and reducing the burden on the electrical grid.

## 🛠 Our Technology
* **Dynamic Charging:** Charging vehicles in motion along highways and urban routes.
* **Stationary & Semi-Dynamic Charging:** Providing power at bus terminals, loading docks, and parking bays.
* **Electreon Flow:** Our advanced software suite that manages energy transfer, monitors infrastructure, and provides smart charging insights.

## 🤝 Why We Value Open Source
Innovation in the transportation sector cannot happen in a vacuum. To achieve net-zero transport emissions, we believe in the power of **partnership and shared knowledge**. 

We value the open-source community for several reasons:
* **Standardization:** We are active members of international bodies like **SAE** and **IEC**. By contributing to and leveraging open standards, we ensure that wireless charging becomes a vehicle-agnostic utility for everyone.
* **Accelerated Innovation:** Open collaboration allows us to solve complex challenges in IoT, embedded systems, and cloud-based energy management more efficiently.
* **Sustainability through Transparency:** Sharing our work helps the wider ecosystem build more sustainable hardware and software, reducing global CO2 emissions together.
